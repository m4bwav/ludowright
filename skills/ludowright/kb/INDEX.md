# ludowright knowledge base

Open only what the task needs. Each file paraphrases the design canon with book and chapter sources (reading notes: `books/plugin-learnings/round3-game-design/` in the owner's workspace; nothing quoted).

- [review-rubric.md](review-rubric.md) and [review-rubric-more.md](review-rubric-more.md): the flaw checks (R-01 to R-68) a review walks, by contract category; read for every review or improve job
- [core-loops-and-goals.md](core-loops-and-goals.md): core loop as a verb, experience goals, player promises, what makes a pitch specific; read for design and for broken-promise findings
- [systems-and-loops.md](systems-and-loops.md): feedback loops, emergence, meaningful decisions, economy patterns; read for degenerate loops and dead choices
- [balance-and-economy.md](balance-and-economy.md): cost curves, transitivity, sources and sinks, progression curves, dominant strategies, which `ldw.py` command answers which question
- [balance-tables.md](balance-tables.md): how to declare a table for `ldw.py ev`, `curve`, `economy` and `sim`
- [uncertainty.md](uncertainty.md): the sources of uncertainty, luck and skill, hidden information, kingmaking and catch-up
- [levels-and-space.md](levels-and-space.md): level and encounter design, pacing graphs, teaching through space
- [ux-and-the-brain.md](ux-and-the-brain.md): perception, attention, memory, motivation, onboarding, usability checks, dark patterns
- [playtesting.md](playtesting.md): playtest plans, session scripts, questionnaire templates, what a playtest can and cannot show
- [procgen.md](procgen.md): when to generate content, authorial control, sameness, evaluating generators, repair passes
- [production-and-scope.md](production-and-scope.md): phases, vertical slice, solo scoping, cutting, production failure patterns
- [game-ai-for-designers.md](game-ai-for-designers.md): AI opponents and NPCs from the design side: readability, fairness, difficulty levers, cheating AI
- [ai-as-design-tool.md](ai-as-design-tool.md): procedural generation, player modelling, simulated playtesting, LLM ideation and critique, and the guards this plugin keeps
- [learning-games.md](learning-games.md): learning and study games: teach versus measure, alignment rows, pre-brief and debrief, the remove-all-rewards test, named reward failures, rules for child players
- [design-drills.md](design-drills.md): text drills for stuck designs: rule remix, seeded random grid, constraint-first ideation, puzzle solvability, strategy-family balance, playtest feedback hygiene
- [feel-handoff.md](feel-handoff.md): spotting feel problems in a review and handing them to game-juice's juice-scan
- [worldbuilding-and-lore.md](worldbuilding-and-lore.md): setting history and lore without contradictions: bookends, exceptions list, zoom levels, outcome-first, three canon checks, open-gap ledger, loaded-question briefs
