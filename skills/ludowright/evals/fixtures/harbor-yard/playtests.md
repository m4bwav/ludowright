# Playtest notes (latest round)

- Ten four-player games. The first player won 4 of 10, close to an even share, so we do not need first-player compensation.
- Tidecallers won 6 of 10. We think that is the testers, not the faction; they were the more experienced group.
- Several players held off raising their first Dock until round 5 or 6 "to keep the Tide bonus". They caught up fast once they started building.
- Nobody bought Rope Ladder in ten games.
- One tester found the Driftwood trick in game 7 and ended a turn with 40 coin. We asked them not to do it again.
- Nobody tried for a Beacon victory. Two players built the Beacon and then could not see what to do next.
- Games felt long by the end; most finished around round 10 on the prototype.
