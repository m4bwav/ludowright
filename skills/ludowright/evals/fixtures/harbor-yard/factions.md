# Factions

Each faction's edge is meant to be worth about 5 resources over a whole game, so they come out even (Pillar 1).

## Tidecallers
Masters of the harbor tides. Gain 2 coin at the start of each of your turns.

## Ironhulls
Old money with a head start. Begin the game with a Dock already built.

## Windrunners
Fast and light. Draw 6 cards instead of 5 on your first turn.
