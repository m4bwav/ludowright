# Economy notes (test fixture)
- Each Mine in play produces 2 gold a turn.
- A typical player has 2 Mines by turn 3 and 4 by turn 6.
- Gold is spent only on buying cards: at most one card a turn, costing 4 to 8.
- Food: each Farm produces 2 a turn; colonists eat 1 each a turn; a new colonist arrives every 3 turns.
