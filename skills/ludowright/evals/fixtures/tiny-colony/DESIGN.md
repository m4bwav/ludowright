# Tiny Colony (test fixture)

An invented two-page design for ludowright's eval suite. Not a real game.

## Pitch
A turn-based colony deckbuilder. Each turn you draw five cards, spend energy (3 a turn) to build and run buildings, and survive an event at the end of the turn. A run lasts 12 turns. The game promises that every turn is a hard trade-off between growing the colony and preparing for the next event.

## Resources
- Energy: 3 at the start of each turn, unspent energy is lost.
- Gold: mines produce it; it buys new cards between turns (cost 4 to 8).
- Food: farms produce it; each colonist eats 1 a turn. Start with 6 food and 3 colonists.

## Rules
1. Draw five cards a turn from your deck; discard your hand at the end of the turn.
2. A building card costs its energy to place and stays in play.
3. Events hit at the end of each turn; damage is reduced by Shield.
4. Gold has no other use than buying cards between turns, and you may buy at most one card a turn.
